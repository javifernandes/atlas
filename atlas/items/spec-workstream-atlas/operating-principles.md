Operating Principles are conceptual frames that shape how Atlas understands systems and designs its experiences.

They are not user workflows by themselves. A principle does not execute like a practice or render like a product experience. It gives context to the practices, experiences, model items, and reconciliation operations that Atlas later makes concrete.
